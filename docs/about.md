# About Me

2012). In 2012, I joined the School of Information Technology at [Halmstad University](http://hh.se/english.5_en.html),
Sweden, as a PhD candidate and finished my PhD (defense in June 2018,
[PhD dissertation](http://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:hh:diva-36699)) with the Center for Applied Intelligent
Systems Research
([CAISR](http://hh.se/english/schoolofinformationtechnology/research/caisrcenterforappliedintelligentsystemsresearch.11375.html)).
My research interests are Autonomous Mobile Robot applications, Machine learning, and Computer Vision.
