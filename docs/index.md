# Saeed Gholami Shahbandi

My research interests are autonomous mobile robots, machine learning, and computer vision.

## About

I have degrees in Electronics and Robotics from Mazandaran University (Iran, 2010) and École centrale de Nantes (France,
2012). In 2012, I joined the School of Information Technology at [Halmstad University](http://hh.se/english.5_en.html),
Sweden, as a PhD candidate. I finished my PhD in June 2018 with the Center for Applied Intelligent Systems Research
([CAISR](http://hh.se/english/schoolofinformationtechnology/research/caisrcenterforappliedintelligentsystemsresearch.11375.html)).
My [dissertation](http://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:hh:diva-36699) explores the interpretation and alignment of 2D
indoor maps.

More on my [research and projects](research.md) and [publications and theses](publications.md).
